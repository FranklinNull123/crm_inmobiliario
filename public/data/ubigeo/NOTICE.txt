Peru UBIGEO reference data

The bundled 2025 district list is based on ubigeo_inei_2025.csv from:
https://github.com/MichaelSuarez0/ubigeos_peru

Source repository license: MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 MichaelSuarez0.
The source identifies the dataset as compiled from the 2025 National Directory
of Regional Governments, Provincial and District Municipalities, and Populated
Centers. District coordinates are reference points, not surveyed property
coordinates.
